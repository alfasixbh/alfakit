Same machine, the queries alone against the SQLite file (PDO, median of 5): `count(*)` 5.6 ms; `order by id limit 25 offset 249975` 3.8 ms; `order by created_at` (indexed) <0.1 ms; `order by unit_price` (unindexed) 40.3 ms; `count(*) where status = 'active'` 3.7 ms; `sku like '%BIG-00424%'` 1.8 ms (stops after 42k rows); prefix range on `sku` <0.1 ms. Seeding 1,000,000 rows: 2.9 s (React playground), 2.8 s (Filament app).

Filament cursor page 2 is not measured: its next cursor is a Livewire call, not a link in the HTML. Pagination modes are switched with `?mode=simple|cursor` read by a closure in each resource.
